# SIDE & MINI-GAMES — concept backlog

Plain-summary idea store for optional/sandbox content. **Vision-stage only** — nothing here is specced or committed; this is the "someday, maybe" shelf. Combat, roster, and the valuation model are the mainline; these are the Elin-style sandbox breadth that makes the world feel lived-in.

Guiding filters (a good side-game usually hits 2+):
- **Reuses something we already built** (the affinity wheel, the 100-unit roster, monster data, the turn/Momentum engine) so it's cheap to add and feels native.
- **Rewards a different brain than combat** — collection, spatial/physics, bluffing, economy — so it's a genuine palate-cleanser, not "combat with a hat."
- **Feeds the mainline loop** — pays out currency, materials, roster XP, or cosmetics without being mandatory or predatory.
- **Single-player-friendly** — strong solo/vs-AI experience first; any social layer is a bonus, never a requirement.

---

## 1. Card game (HIGH interest — owner wants this)

A collectible card mini-game reachable from taverns/towns, played vs NPCs for stakes. The genre reference points, and what to steal from each:

- **Triple Triad (Final Fantasy VIII)** — 3×3 grid, cards with four edge-numbers, capture by placing a higher-facing number against a neighbor. Elegant, fast, deeply readable. Steal: the **grid-capture core** and the joy of *winning cards off opponents* as the reward loop.
- **Gwent (The Witcher 3)** — row-based, multi-round (best of three), hand-management and "when do I pass / bleed a round" bluffing. Steal: the **round/economy tension** and that you commit a finite hand across rounds — resource attrition, not just tempo.
- **Pazaak (KotOR)** — blackjack-to-20 with a pre-built side-deck of modifier cards you play to nudge totals. Push-your-luck + a small deckbuild. Steal: the **side-deck as your "build"** and the tight risk/bust math.

**Our card game — direction to explore (pick/blend later):**
- Cards are the **100 roster units + monsters**, so the card game doubles as a codex/collection flex. Card stats derive from unit identity (a Legendary card is rarer/stronger; affinity shows on the card).
- **Affinity wheel as the capture/counter rule** — Red beats Green, Blue beats Red, etc., mirrors the combat wheel, so knowledge transfers both ways. Gold/Dark are the rare off-wheel "wild" cards.
- Acquisition: win cards off NPCs (Triple-Triad style), or craft/earn them from the sandbox. Non-predatory: **no card packs for real money**; you earn the collection by playing.
- Progression hook: a card collection could grant small, flavorful account-wide bonuses, or just be its own completionist itch. Keep it optional.
- Open question: one card game or a small **arcade of them** (a Triad-like AND a Pazaak-like) so different towns/regions have their local game?

## 2. Monster Arena (HIGH interest — owner wants this)

**Dragon Quest Monster Arena** style: **catch, manage, and train monsters, then bet/enter them into arena brackets for rewards.** A management + collection loop parallel to the main party.

- **Catch/recruit** monsters out in the sandbox (tame after battle? traps? befriend via items?). They're distinct from the 100 recruitable units — a second collection axis.
- **Manage/train** — monsters level, maybe breed or fuse (Elin/DQ lineage), have their own affinity + a simplified kit. Stable/pen management scratches the sandbox itch.
- **Arena** — enter them into tiered tournaments (rank brackets, DQ-style). Fights can run on the **existing combat engine** (huge reuse win) with the player as coach/better rather than direct controller, or a lighter auto-resolve.
- **Rewards** — arena winnings feed currency/materials/rare monster eggs; climbing ranks unlocks tougher brackets and prestige.
- Hooks nicely into a **breeding/fusion** system later (combine two monsters → a stronger/rarer one), which is deep sandbox content on its own.
- Design tension to preserve: it should reward *husbandry and matchmaking smarts* (reading the bracket, countering with the right monster/affinity), echoing the "chess for chess players" ethos in a collection frame.

## 3. Sports mini-games (owner: basketball + golf)

Physics/timing games that use a totally different skill than turn-based combat — the classic JRPG resort/festival detour.

- **Basketball** — arcade hoops (timing-meter shooting, maybe a horse/3-point-contest mode, or light 3v3). Could cast **roster units as players** with stats nudging their shooting/speed so your team choice matters. Reward: tokens for the local prize shop.
- **Golf** — power/accuracy-meter golf (think Mario Golf / classic power-arc). Chill, session-based, very re-playable. Courses themed to the world's regions. Optional: light RPG layer where clubs/balls are gear.
- Both are **self-contained, low-stakes, high-charm** — good "take a break from the dungeon" content and natural festival/holiday event fits.
- Keep controls readable and forgiving; these are palate-cleansers, not skill walls.

## 4. Fishing (owner wants this)

The near-mandatory cozy sandbox staple — a timing/patience loop that's cheap to build, endlessly re-playable, and connects the whole economy. Reference points: Stardew Valley's cast → bite → reel-mini-bar tension; classic JRPG fishing (FFXV, Breath of Fire) for the "hook a legendary catch" thrill.

**Direction to explore:**
- **Core loop** — cast into a fishing spot, wait for a bite (patience/timing), then a small reel mini-game (keep-the-fish-in-the-bar, or a tension/line-snap meter) whose difficulty scales with the fish's rarity.
- **Catch table by location + time** — different waters (rivers, coasts, dungeon pools, region-themed lakes) hold different fish; time-of-day / weather / bait shift the odds. Rare and "legendary" fish give the completionist chase.
- **Feeds the sandbox web** — catches become **cooking ingredients** (buff consumables for combat), **crafting/alchemy** materials, **monster-training food** (ties into the Arena), or straight sell-for-coin. This is the connective tissue that makes fishing *matter*, not just a diversion.
- **Gear layer (optional)** — rods, lines, bait, lures as light equipment that improves bite rate / catch quality; a small progression without being grindy.
- **Codex/collection** — a fish journal to complete, per region; pairs naturally with the card-game-as-codex and monster-collection themes.
- **Charm hooks** — junk catches (boots, bottles), treasure-chest pulls, a "one that got away" tale system; keep it low-stakes and forgiving.
- **Possible crossover** — catchable water *monsters* that feed the Monster Arena, blurring fishing and monster-catching for a rare thrill.

## 5. Casino / Gold Saucer hub (owner wants this)

A dedicated entertainment hub that **houses several mini-games under one roof** with a shared token economy and a prize shop — the classic FF Gold Saucer / DQ casino energy. The hub is the *frame*; the games inside are the content, which lets us add minis incrementally.

**Direction to explore:**
- **Shared token economy** — earn tokens by playing any hub game, spend them at a **prize exchange** for cosmetics, rare items, materials, or the occasional exclusive unit/monster/card. Tokens are earned in-game only.
- **Games to house (pick/grow over time):**
  - **Slots** — themed reels, simple and punchy; good "background" game while chatting to NPCs.
  - **Dice / craps-like** — push-your-luck betting on rolls.
  - **Wheel of fortune / spin-to-win** — daily-spin cadence, low effort, small dopamine.
  - **Card tables** — the card game (section 1) naturally lives here too; blackjack/poker-likes fit the room.
  - **Betting parlor** — wager tokens on the **Monster Arena** brackets and the **racing** results, tying hubs together.
- **NON-PREDATORY — hard rule:** tokens are earned by playing, **never bought with real money**; prizes are cosmetics/items, not power you can't get elsewhere; **no loot-box-with-a-price-tag mechanics.** This is the project's stance and the casino is where it's most tempting to violate — so it's called out explicitly here.
- **Charm hooks** — a flashy hub map, a token-jingle, "high roller" rep tiers that unlock nicer prize tiers or private tables, a resident hustler NPC to beat.
- **Reuse:** the hub is mostly UI + economy glue; several housed games (card game, arena betting, race betting) already exist elsewhere, so the hub *aggregates* rather than builds from scratch.

## 6. Mount racing (owner wants this — chocobo-style)

Chocobo-racing-style mount competition — **pilot or bet on mounts around a track.** A physics/timing + light-management game with an obvious collection/breeding tail.

**Direction to explore:**
- **Two ways to play** — **pilot** a mount yourself (steering, stamina/sprint management, item pickups on track à la Chocobo Racing / Mario Kart-lite), or **bet** on AI races in the casino/parlor for a lower-effort loop.
- **Mount collection** — catch/tame/raise mounts (parallels the Monster Arena husbandry loop; could even share tech). Mounts have stats — top speed, acceleration, stamina, cornering — that make choice matter.
- **Breeding / lineage** — race winners breed better foals (classic chocobo pedigree chase); deep optional collection content, pairs with the Arena's breeding/fusion if we build one shared "creature husbandry" system.
- **Tracks** — region-themed courses with hazards, shortcuts, terrain that favors certain mount stats; time-trial mode for solo mastery.
- **Rewards** — race winnings + casino tokens, rare mount eggs/bloodlines, cosmetics (tack, colors), and mounts double as **overworld traversal** (sandbox utility beyond the mini-game).
- **Reuse & synergy:** shares a husbandry/breeding backbone with the Monster Arena; feeds and is fed by the casino betting parlor; mounts serve the exploration layer too, so the system pays off in more than one place.
- Design tension to preserve: reward *stable management + track/matchup reading*, not just twitch — keep it in the "smart choices" family with the rest of the game.

## 7. Other ideas worth a look (unranked, for later triage)

- **Cooking / alchemy crafting mini** — combine ingredients (from fishing, farming, monster drops) via a small timing or recipe-discovery game; outputs consumables that feed combat. Ties the whole sandbox together.
- **Farming / ranch plot** — Elin/Stardew-adjacent; grow materials, passive income, feeds cooking + monster training. Core sandbox connective tissue.
- **Rhythm / bard mini** — timing game for tavern performances; earns coin + reputation, flavorful.
- **Puzzle board (dungeon-lite)** — sokoban/tile puzzles as optional dungeon rooms for bonus loot; reuses map tech, rewards a spatial brain.
- **Tower defense skirmish** — deploy roster units on lanes vs waves; reuses unit kits in a fresh frame; good for a limited-time event.
- **Auction house / merchant sim mini** — buy-low/sell-high trading loop across towns with fluctuating prices; feeds the economy brain, pairs with farming/fishing/crafting outputs.

## Cross-cutting notes

- **Reward philosophy:** side-games pay out into the main economy (currency, mats, roster/monster XP, cosmetics) but are **never mandatory** and **never monetized predatorily** — this is the project's core stance. Earn-by-playing only.
- **Engine reuse is the cheat code:** the Arena and Tower-Defense ideas ride the existing combat engine; the card game rides the affinity wheel; sports/fishing/golf need small new physics/timing systems but are self-contained.
- **Build order suggestion (when the time comes):** card game and Monster Arena first (highest owner interest + heaviest reuse), then **fishing** as the first sandbox-connective piece (cheap, cozy, feeds cooking/economy/monster-training) alongside a sports mini for charm/variety. The **casino hub** slots in naturally once 2–3 housed games exist (it aggregates rather than builds), and **mount racing** pairs with the Monster Arena's husbandry/breeding backbone — build them near each other to share that tech. Farming/cooking connective tissue rounds it out once the core loop is fun.
- **Priority right now:** none. Mainline combat + roster + valuation model comes first; this doc just makes sure the ideas aren't lost.
